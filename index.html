How It Works

4 simple steps — fully automated

01

Input

Take a photo, paste text, scan a QR code, or upload a screenshot.

02

Extract

Yene Verify finds the receipt URL or transaction ID automatically.

03

Verify

The platform fetches real payment data from the bank's server.

04

Result

Verified or Fake, with full details.

Real Example — Photo Method

How a shop owner verifies a customer's payment

1
Customer says they paid 5,500 ETB via CBE and shows their phone
2
Shop owner takes a photo of the customer's bank confirmation screen
3
Yene Verify reads the text from the photo using OCR and AI
4
App fetches the real receipt from CBE's server and matches the details
5
Verified: Abebe sent 5,500 ETB — payment is genuine
